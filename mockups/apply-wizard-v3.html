    v3 — what changed and why

    • No DOB, no street address. Maya receives the OCR values in STATE (state.py:243) and can confirm/correct them in one turn (resolve_name_mismatch). A form field would be duplicate work.
    • No documents in the wizard. They stay in chat. Screen 5 only prepares people — which is where the 35%→10% first-attempt rejection gain comes from.
    • Credit at step 3 with soft-pull copy. Below 650 continues to review — never an auto-reject (KB C-01).
    • Timezone needs no permission. Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone — no prompt, no GPS. Falls back to a manual picker.
    • Contact channel is the LAST step. contact_pref is required by leads_sync.py:184 — a lead whose value is not email|whatsapp|telegram is not routed for outreach at all. It sits last because the choice decides what the final button can do: picking WhatsApp reveals "Message us now".
    • WhatsApp links to wa.me/13183147693 (KB FY-04, the only number on file — marked unverified in the KB). One constant; every link follows it.
    • Availability tiers. Faster verification response moves the file along sooner and is reflected in the rate. Labels only — no figures, because no payment tier exists in the KB yet (FY-10 states a flat $1,000/month).
    • Availability is the job. Speed + hours + timezone, read back as one concrete promise they can be held to.
    • Payment wording: "paid monthly" plus "faster availability is reflected in your rate". No amount and no start date promised (T-04 says 3–5 weeks to first pay).
    • Mobile: 16px inputs, 52px targets, safe-area insets, reduced-motion honoured.
