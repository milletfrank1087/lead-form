      Why it's built this way

      • 5 steps, not 13 fields. Baymard's benchmark finds total field count hurts completion far more than step count — but only if each step stays effortless. Every step here asks for two things.
      • Optional fields are disclosed, not deleted. City, state, message and referral sit behind one click, so they cost the 90% nothing.
      • Native radios, custom skin. Employment and credit score are option cards, so it's one tap with no keyboard — and arrow-key navigation works for free.
      • Validation on blur, then live. Errors persist until fixed, announce through a polite live region, and use aria-invalid + aria-describedby. Never colour alone.
      • Mobile specifics. 16px inputs (stops iOS zoom), 54px targets, autocomplete on every field, the right inputmode per field, and safe-area insets.
      • One hero moment. Everything else is restrained, so the ink wipe and drawn check on submit land hard.
      • Motion is opt-out. Honours prefers-reduced-motion; the Motion toggle here lets you preview that.
      • Field names match your current payload exactly — porting this back into Apply.tsx is mechanical.
